# Working with the owner

## How they communicate

- **Messages:** short and casual, often several requests in one message, with typos. Read every clause; each one
  is a task.
- **Praise and criticism:** they say clearly what they love ("that dark wizard was a lot of fun") and what they
  don't ("sticks out like a sore thumb").
- **Explaining:** when they say "get it?", they're describing a feeling. Think about how the thing would look or
  behave in real life, then build that.
- **Ambition:** "keep going", "much much better" and "get to work" mean they expect substantial work, not a token
  change.

## Before you start

1. **List every request in the message,** and plan to address each one.
2. **Interpret by their rules:** physical plausibility, counterplay, real art, consistency, brevity. When two
   readings are possible, pick the one that fits those, and say which reading you chose. Force Wall versus
   Barrier was an example.
3. **Ask only when the answer changes what you build** and you can't infer it. Never ask for permission to do
   what they plainly asked.

## While working

- **Keep them posted** with a short line of what you're doing during long stretches.
- **Don't touch unrelated systems.** Don't rename things they know (spell names, keys) without being asked.
- **Their files:**
  - Don't delete their original files (`art_source/`, `art/wizard_parts/`,
    `Wizard-character-FreeSprite.zip`).
  - Don't commit or push unless asked. Check `git status` first: the owner may have uncommitted work.
- **Downloads** go into a scratch folder outside the repo. Read the licence before using anything. Add every new
  asset to `art/CREDITS.md`.

## Reporting back

- **Lead with what changed,** one item per request, in their words. Then what you couldn't verify. Then, if
  useful, short recommendations.
- **Be honest:**
  - "I couldn't listen to the sounds."
  - "I didn't play it by hand."
  - "Two run frames still show a faint line."
  - Never claim something works that you only assumed.
- **Format:** plain, skimmable markdown. No walls of text, no internal jargon, no internal function names unless
  they help.
- **Sources:** list links to any asset sources you used.

## Things they've never asked for (so don't add them unprompted)

- Difficulty settings, achievements, analytics, story text, tutorials full of text.
- Cartoon or "cute" anything.
- More UI text.
