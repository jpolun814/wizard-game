# World generation

A world is 360 by 170 tiles, made from a seed (four incantation words, or random when none is spoken).

## Layout
- **The surface** is the first hall and the start. It is open sky over rolling ground the width of the world, with a ruined castle on it. Under it lie the caves. Nothing on the surface is a trap: a trench across it would cut it in two, so the only ways down are two steep tunnels, one at each end of the world.
- **The castle** (136 columns, `WorldBuilder._castle`) is solid stone with rooms carved out of it on three floors, so nothing floats. From the west: a gate and entry hall, a 45-degree stair up to a room on the first floor, a stair up to the great hall and two more rooms on the second floor (under the keep), a stair down to a room on the first floor, a stair down and a back door onto the plain; the way through goes up and over (`test_world.gd` walks it both ways). Between rooms stand walls with a doorway four rows tall and a wooden `CastleDoor` in it: it is a front-on door hinged on its left that is drawn narrower as it swings open when anyone comes near, shuts again 1.2 s after they go, and shut it is as solid as the wall, so it stops shots and spells. The towers, keep and the walls under the floors carry arrow-slit windows. The rooms are painted with the church backdrop (`art/backgrounds/church/hall.png`), a little under the stone round them so no sky shows at a stair's corners; the door is cut from LPC Animated Doors (`tools/prepare_doors.py`).
- **The caves** are 9 to 12 halls (in practice 5 to 8) placed anywhere under the surface. Each has its own width, height and floor: rolling hills and valleys, now and then a mesa, a lake, a pool of lava with rock pillars standing in it, a dry pit, and ziggurats of rock to climb over. Nothing floats.
- **Tunnels** join halls. A gentle tunnel climbs a row in two columns, a steep one a row in one (45 degrees, the most a walker can climb). A hall has one tunnel on each side. Three extra loop tunnels are added between halls that sit near each other.
- The boss hall is the farthest by tunnels (at least 3). Every tunnel has an empty gate slot.
- There are no one-way platforms and no shafts: vertical travel is by slope.

## Code
- `scripts/world/world_graph.gd` places the halls and tunnels.
- `scripts/world/world_builder.gd` starts all rock and carves it, then adds the castle and formations. `reachable()` is a walk, step, fall and jump search (with lava) that every hall marker must pass.
- `scripts/terrain.gd` (tiled mode) traces the boundary between air and rock into closed outlines, spreads them evenly, rounds them off and thins them. That gives smooth hills and slopes like the waiting room. The rock is one textured layer, the caves are the outlines filled over it with a dark rim, grass is one mesh along every stretch facing up, the sky is gradient bands over the air above the surface, the castle is a brick layer with the church backdrop painted inside its hall, and rock standing in the air (the lava pillars) is drawn in front of the pools.
- Collision is one concave outline per cave. A concave outline has no inside, so a body that gets into rock is not found by a physics query: `World` asks the tiles instead, stops teleports short of rock, and puts the wizard back on the last firm ground if they end up in rock or fall out of the world.

## Tests
- `tests/test_world.gd -- seeds=N pic=S out=DIR`: determinism, every hall reachable, boss depth, and a negative check (walls across the surface must cut halls off).
- `tests/showcase.tscn -- scenario=cave`: snapshots of the start, the castle, a lake and lava, and the steepest tunnel walked with only the walk key.

## Update: a sprawling, chunked world

- **Size:** 1800 x 380 tiles, built for four players dropped in different places, each with halls of their own around them.
- **Surface** (`SurfaceGen`): the graveyard and castle in the middle, two of mountains, swamp, tundra and ashlands on each side, with water and lava pools.
- **Halls** (`WorldGraph`): about 90-130 caverns, great halls and vaults. Every 80 or so columns of surface has a way down, and each is the start of a system of halls that grows from it. Systems are joined by tunnels between hall ends and by steep ramps from the middle of a floor down to a hall below, so there are many routes. `spawns` holds four ways down spread along the surface.
- **Locks and gates:** up to fourteen areas behind gates (`MAX_LOCKS`; at least three rooms each, at most 40% of the world) (never a way down from the surface). The widest hall of each holds a guard, the biggest holds the boss, and keys lie outside every lock. A gate is not a door: it is a place that cannot be crossed without a spell, and any spell that plausibly beats it should. Each lock gets one of seven, chosen in `WorldGraph._lock` from what the passage has room for (`_gate_fits`) and spread so a world has a mix: a **lava chasm** (20 columns, too wide to jump even hasted; crossed with anything that bridges, carries or lifts), a **thorn field** (28 columns of the Bramble spell's own thorns: at a walk it costs more than a whole stamina bar, under the Chant of Swift Feet less; fire clears it a thicket at a time) and a **wall** (a block 8 rows high with room cut over it; climbed or lifted over). `WorldBuilder._make_gate` carves them and returns, for the test, what a spell would change (`solve`) and the tiles the thorns cover (`thorns`). Which spells beat which gate is not decided in code yet, and pages are not placed anywhere a gate's counter is guaranteed. `Gate` (a stone door) is no longer used.
- **Tiles** (`TileGrid`): one byte per tile in a packed array, not a Dictionary.
- **Rendering and collision** (`Contour`, `RockWorld`): the edge between rock and air is traced into smooth outlines once (fast, row by row). `RockWorld` cuts them into 32-tile chunks: each chunk is its own canvas item, made when a player comes near and freed when they leave, and collision is one concave shape per chunk. The minimap is a single picture.
- **Checks:** `tests/test_world.gd` checks that every hall is reachable from the start and from each of the four starting places, that gates really lock (the locked halls cannot be reached with the gates in place, and all can with each gate taken away), that keys exist, and that the world sprawls (ways down in every biome, loops, starting places far apart). `tests/perf.tscn` runs the wizard along a world and prints frame costs.

- **Water and breath:** under water the wizard has `Player.BREATH` (8) seconds of air, shown as bubbles over the head. Once it is gone the most stamina falls at `DROWN_RATE` (3) per second until they surface, and that loss comes back only the usual ways. The Chant of the Deep Breath (`sub aqua spiro`) holds the wizard's breath for 45 seconds, counting down from the chant: no air is used up under water and gas does no harm. It cannot be said under water, so it cannot be renewed there. Only the part of a body below a pond's surface is coloured (`wet.gdshader` for the wizard, `mob.gdshader` for creatures).

- **More gates:** besides the lava chasm, the thorn field and the wall there are four more, made in `WorldBuilder._make_gate` and grown into the world by `World`. A **flood** is the chasm pit filled to the brim with water (beaten by what bridges, freezes or floats on water). An **ice plug** (`IcePlug`) fills the passage floor to roof for three columns: fire melts it, a blow from stone breaks it. A **dark stretch** (`SpikeBed`) has strips of spikes no one sees until a light (a flare, any fire) falls on them; they cut for 30 each, and a jump clears one. A **poison gas** bank (`GasBank`, four banks of eight columns) poisons everyone in it for 15 every quarter second (a Barrier keeps it off); wind clears it a bank at a time and fire burns it off. A **piranha pit** (`Piranhas`) is a long (20 columns), shallow (2 rows) pit of water that a wizard can wade but not jump: its fish swim to anyone whose middle is under the surface and bite for 15 every 0.6 s. Above it, on ice, lily pads or a bridge, no one is bitten. Lightning in the water is the only thing that kills them: a Spark strike within 150 px of a fish along the pit (`SparkStrike` calls `Piranhas.shock`). A **sentinel** (`Sentinel`) is a hooded statue on a passage's floor that shoots a 40-damage bolt a second at whoever it sees within 700 px, after half a second of seeing them. It sees a decoy before the wizard and wears it down 8 per shot (the shot lands at once, so no bolt flies on past it) (the decoy has 40 health); smoke and an eclipse blind it, a Barrier stops its bolts, rock hides from it. A Spark under a roof now comes down from the roof (not from the sky above the rock). Nothing marks a gate: no light, no sign. `test_world.gd` models ice as rock and the rest as impassable until solved.
- **Finding locked areas:** up to fourteen are made, and `WorldGraph._lock` makes sure that one lies near each starting place (`NEAR_LOCK`, counted in passages, walking the surface to another way down counts 1 per 40 columns), using areas of one or two halls if need be. The test prints how far the nearest gate is from every starting place.
- **Weather and castle:** `Weather` rides on the camera and fills the view with snow (mountains), a blizzard (tundra), ash with rising embers (ashlands) or spores (swamp), fading out underground and between biomes. The castle's wall is `art/tiles/castle_brick.png`, made by `tools/prepare_castle_brick.py` in the colours of the hall's stonework, and the hall's picture runs along and back so it never jumps.
- **Pits slope out:** a lava chasm or flood pit slopes gently down (a row for every two columns) from the side a walker comes in by, so anyone who falls in can walk back out; the far side is sheer. Lava lies in the deep part only. The F1 panel has a "Go to a gate" button for each kind of gate in the world (`World.go_to_gate`): it sets the wizard down just outside the next gate of that kind. A flare goes out in water. The bonfire is built on a ring of logs and stones, beside the wizard.
- **Pages:** in a world only the starter pages are known: one attack, one move and one defence, each drawn by the seed from its own list (`Pages.ATTACKS`, `MOVES`, `DEFENCES`), and the incantations of the bonfire and the rift. A spell or chant is cast only once its page is found (`Pages`, `PagePickup`); an unknown one misfires with the same "Misfire" as a shape that matches nothing. The other 36 pages are laid from the seed, one or two to a hall, lying flat on the floor: a page that may open a gate only in halls outside the locked areas, so the way on never shuts behind itself; pages that open no gate may also lie in a locked area (one in each, never the dark wizard's). Nothing is held back for the dark wizard yet (`Pages.STASH` is 0). Standing on a page shows "Press Tab to read"; Tab reads it into the spellbook and opens the book at it. The page is cut from the book's parchment (`tools/prepare_page.py`). The book shows only the pages found. The F1 panel's "All spells" button learns every page without taking any off the map. The waiting room knows everything. With Insight on, hovering a dark wizard lists the spells it casts, each in its school's colour (`WizardBrain.KNOWN`). `test_world.gd` checks the layout; `showcase.gd scenario=pages` and `scenario=extras` play the pickup, doors, steered decoy, F1 teleports, flare, bonfire and Insight.
